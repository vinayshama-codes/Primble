Here is the feedback from COMMERCIAL INSURANCE APPLICATION - ACORD 125 that was populated by Primble:
Overall assessment
This ACORD 125 is not ready for client signature or carrier submission. Primble correctly captured some basic policy information, but it also inserted carrier data, agency data, policy boilerplate, coverage limits and current-policy information into unrelated applicant fields.
The underlying policy supports only these active lines:
Commercial General Liability — $3,954
Commercial Inland Marine — $300
Commercial Auto — $2,991
Commercial Umbrella — $3,418
Total package premium — $10,663
The declarations expressly show no Commercial Property, Crime/Fidelity or Workers’ Compensation coverage.
Page 1: Header, coverage and applicant information
1. Producer information needs correction
Primble populated:
Fax: 303-996-7800
Email: ERIN ROYAL
No producer street address
“ERIN ROYAL” is not an email address. The fax number appears copied from the producer’s phone and cannot be confirmed. The producer’s complete address should include 9780 S. Meridian Blvd., Suite 400, Englewood, CO 80112-6072.
Fix: Add the street address, enter Erin’s actual email, and leave fax blank unless verified.
2. The application date appears copied from the policy effective date
The application completion date is 07/15/2025, exactly matching the policy effective date.
Fix: Use the actual date the application is completed. If this is a current renewal application, the proposed dates must also be updated because the displayed term ended July 15, 2026.
3. Carrier, product and policy-number fields are internally inconsistent
The header identifies:
Carrier: Employers Mutual Casualty Company
Product: Commercial Auto
Policy number: 6E7-40-02---26
But the form also checks GL, Inland Marine and Umbrella. Those sections do not all have the same insurer or policy number. For example, the GL coverage is issued by EMC Property & Casualty Company under BBC7263-26.
The value in “Program Code” is also a full carrier form/trailer string, not an identifiable agency program code.
Fix: Define how Primble handles multi-policy, multi-carrier packages. Do not present the Auto policy number as though it governs every selected line.
4. Transaction status needs clarification
Both Issue Policy and Bound are populated, but no bound date is provided.
Fix: Select the appropriate status. If Bound remains selected, add the actual bound date.
5. Remove incorrect lines of business
Remove:
Commercial Property
Crime
Cyber and Privacy
“Commercial Auto” under Other
“Commercial Liability Umbrella” under Other
The two “Other” descriptions merely duplicate the standard Business Auto and Umbrella selections.
Cyber-related terms in the policy do not establish a standalone Cyber policy. In fact, the GL forms include a Cyber Incident and Data Privacy exclusion notice.
6. Correct the attachment selections
Keep:
Vehicle Schedule
Electronic Data Processing Section
Remove unless genuinely attached:
Driver Information Schedule
Open Cargo Section
Add:
Contractors Supplement, if the generated ACORD 186 is part of the package.
The policy provides a vehicle schedule and EDP/computer coverage, but it does not establish Open Cargo coverage.
7. The $3,954 deposit is incorrect
Primble entered:
Deposit: $3,954
Estimated total: $10,663
The $3,954 amount is the entire GL premium, not an identified deposit.
Fix: Remove the $3,954 deposit unless billing documents confirm an actual deposit. Retain $10,663 as the policy/package premium.
The policy supports Direct Bill, but it does not necessarily support using “DIRECT BILL” as the billing plan, payment plan and payment method simultaneously.
8. The FEIN is wrong
The FEIN field contains 0482854. That is the EMC account number, not the insured’s FEIN.
Fix: Delete it and obtain Orbin’s actual FEIN.
9. Insured phone and website are wrong or unsupported
The insured phone is 303-996-7800, which is the producer’s phone number. The website is www.emcins.com, which appears to be the insurer’s website, not Orbin Contracting’s.
Fix: Obtain the insured’s phone and website from the client. Do not substitute producer or carrier information.
10. Remove incomplete Other Named Insured records
The form includes:
A second LLC indicator
Partial FEIN 84-
A member/manager range of 0–25
Another partial address record without a corresponding named insured
These are not usable records.
Fix: Remove them unless an actual Other Named Insured is identified by full legal name, address and entity type.

Product-level fixes for Primble
This form exposes six rules the extraction logic needs:
Never use an account number as a FEIN.
Never place producer or carrier contact information into applicant fields.
Never convert policy boilerplate into applicant-history answers.
Never convert limits or exposures into revenue, employee counts or building area.
Never auto-populate a signature.
Require field-level provenance: every populated value should show the source page, source label and confidence.
